# Humanizer pass (every post, reply and comment)

## Cut what gives away machine writing
- Empty openers: "In today's world", "Great post!", "Love this".
- Formulas: "not just X, but Y"; three adjectives in a row; always three items; a rhetorical question answered right after.
- Words: leverage, delve, robust, seamless, game-changer, thrilled, passionate, landscape, unlock, journey.
- Dashes in the middle of a sentence, arrows, unicode bold, more than one emoji.
- A closing moral ("In the end, what matters is...").

## Add what only a person would write
- One concrete detail of theirs: a number, a tool, who asked, what went wrong.
- Sentences of different lengths. A short one on its own now and then.
- An opinion with a side. Polite disagreement is fine.
- The person's own way of speaking, from `VOICE.md`.

## Final test
- Read it aloud: would they say this to a colleague? If not, rewrite.
- Swap their name for someone else's: does the text still work? Then it is generic and needs a detail.
- Compare with the last three published texts: same opening, shape or ending? Change it.
- Comments and replies: two to four sentences.
