# Hook library

First-line shapes to fill with the person's own content. Keep the STRUCTURE, never someone else's words.

| Shape | The person's example (already used) | Result |
|---|---|---|

Shapes to try:
- News plus "that was me": [Company] launched [thing that does X]. At [place], X was me.
- Scale in short numbers, then the turn: [time] I did [role]. [N] people. [N] projects. I did not [the obvious].
- Origin: Before [what I do now], I was [something else].
- Small concrete case: A few years ago I did [small job] for [client].
- The question from the person who decides: [Title] asked me straight: "..."
- Side by side: same data, two rules, two results.
- The mistake: I [did something wrong] for [time]. What changed was [detail].
